Reading Environment and History through Tree Rings

An introductory dendrochronology course in eight 90-minute lessons. No prior measurement or statistics training is required. Lesson 1 requires no preparation; its videos are for after-class review and independent study.

The videos are older slide-reading prototypes and may not match the current materials.
