# Correction to the assessment method
For participants in Reading Environment and History through Tree Rings

## Assessment
The course grade is based solely on the final report. Classroom participation and in-class learning activity do not contribute to the grade.

This notice corrects the assessment statement in the existing syllabus, which refers to both in-class learning activity and the report.

## Final report
Choose a question related to dendrochronology and write either a research plan or a literature review. The report should be 2–4 A4 pages and cite at least three references. Figures and tables may be included. Explain the evidence, comparisons, and the limits of the conclusion or proposed plan.

The accompanying material presents a proposed rubric with five criteria worth 20 points each: the question, materials and methods or literature selection, comparison and reasoning, sources and citation, and conclusions and limitations. See the [assignment and proposed rubric](L08_assignment_rubric_en_v1.pdf) for the performance levels and further details.

## Submission
The existing syllabus specifies submission on paper. Check the course instructions for the submission deadline, location and other details. The learning centre does not have a printer; check the instructions about printing in advance.
