# Chapter 5: revised figures and sources

## 23　Horyuji: locate Nara before interpreting the central pillar

Locate Nara within Japan, then compare Horyuji and Yakushiji. Markers indicate approximate temple locations, not sampling positions. Mitsutani (2001) reported felling in 594 for the Horyuji pagoda central pillar, identifying three outer layers beyond the measured series ending in 591. Building-wide construction requires additional evidence about timber use, written records and architecture. Consult the original report.

## 24　Yakushiji East Pagoda: location and timber dates

Yakushiji is in Nishinokyo, Nara. Locate it separately from Horyuji before comparing the East Pagoda timbers. The lower central pillar has a surviving outer ring dated 719, with sapwood but no bark. Two first-storey boards retain bark and date to 729 and 730. These dates do not have identical meanings. Geographic proximity does not establish common construction history; combine tree-ring, written and architectural evidence.
