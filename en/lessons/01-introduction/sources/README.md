## Slide 14: measured Yanase sugi series

Yonenobu, H., Hoshino, Y. and Ohyama, M. (2021). Yanase sugi (Cryptomeria japonica) tree-ring chronology 1768-2004. Japanese Journal of Historical Botany 29(1), 15-19. The original figure was reused from the supplied earlier lecture, slide 34, with axes and scales preserved.

Grey lines show individual series and the thick black line the combined chronology. The lower panel shows agreement in year-to-year changes. The ring-width axis is logarithmic. These are measurements from 18 discs, not simulated teaching data.

[Original article and PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/hisbot/29/1/29_15/_article/-char/en)

[Learning crossdating: University of Arizona](https://www.ltrr.arizona.edu/eg/skelsurvey.html)
