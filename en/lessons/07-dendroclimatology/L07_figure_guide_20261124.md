# Chapter 7: revised figures and sources

## 11　ENSO: locate the equatorial Pacific and Japan

Locate Japan, the equator and the Americas. El Niño involves warmer central and eastern equatorial Pacific sea-surface temperatures coupled with atmospheric changes. The dashed arrow represents remote influence through atmospheric circulation, not ocean water travelling to Japan. Warmer Japanese winters are a tendency, not a deterministic forecast.

## 12　AO: locate Japan in a north-polar view

The Arctic is at the centre; locate Japan, Eurasia and North America. Positive AO has lower pressure anomalies over the Arctic and higher anomalies in middle latitudes. Signs reverse in the negative phase, which can be associated with cold-air outbreaks. Regional circulation and other factors also matter; AO alone does not determine Japanese winter weather.

- [Source 1](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/know/yougo_hp/haichi4.html)
- [Source 2](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

## 13　PDO: view the North Pacific from Japan to North America

Locate the North Pacific east of Japan and the North American coast. Positive PDO is characterized by cool central-basin and warmer coastal SST anomalies. Coloured regions are explanatory, not measured boundaries or values. PDO is related to ENSO but is not identical, nor does it reverse on an exact ten-year schedule. Specify the reconstructed region and season.

- [Source 1](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/kaiyou/data/db/climate/knowledge/pac/pacific_decadal.html)
- [Source 2](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/)

## 25　The same widths produce different indices with different reference curves

The upper plot shows raw widths and two reference curves on a logarithmic width axis. The lower plot divides the same widths by each reference, producing unitless indices. The supplied curve is not a guaranteed biological truth. A short moving mean can follow slower shared variation, reducing it in the resulting index. Neither smoothness nor a visually clean result alone establishes the appropriate method.

- [Source 1](https://cloud.r-project.org/web/packages/dplR/dplR.pdf)
- [Source 2](https://doi.org/10.1177/095968369500500211)

## 26　Chronology span is not the same as preserved timescale

Overlapping short series can span about 200 years, yet per-series standardization can make slow common variation difficult to separate from individual growth trends. This is the segment length curse. A long chronology does not automatically preserve long-period climate variation. Avoid treating individual series length as an absolute universal cutoff; standardization strategies and sample structure matter.

- [Source 1](https://doi.org/10.1177/095968369500500211)
