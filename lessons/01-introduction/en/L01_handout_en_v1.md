# Lesson 1 Dating the past and reconstructing environments

Dendrochronology  Student handout

90 minutes. Corresponds to slides L01_S01–L01_S27.

## Learning objectives

- Choose a method for the sample and event, and explain what it dates.

- Distinguish age uncertainty from temporal resolution.

- Separate dating an environmental archive from interpreting its proxy information.

## Methods and targets

| Method | Sample or clock | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dendrochronology | Ring-pattern matching | Ring formation versus felling and use |
| Radiocarbon | Radiocarbon in biological material | Calibration, contamination and carbon source |
| K–Ar / Ar–Ar, U–Th | Isotopes in minerals or carbonates | Clock closure, retention and initial components |
| Luminescence | Signals reset by light or heat | Resetting and burial environment |
| Archaeomagnetism | Magnetism related to firing and cooling | Regional reference and later movement |

## Age notation

1000 ± 30 BP illustrates a conventional radiocarbon age, referenced to 1950. Calibration relates it to calendar ages such as cal BP or cal BCE/CE. Subtracting an uncalibrated BP value from 1950 does not establish a calendar year. Crossdated rings receive verified calendar years.

Numerical examples and schematic diagrams are teaching examples, not measurements from actual sites.

# Key terms for Lesson 1

Use Japanese and English terms together. Consult the shared bilingual glossary for terms used in later lessons.

| Japanese | English | Brief explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 年代測定 | dating | Placing a sample or event in time. |
| 編年 | chronology | The temporal ordering of evidence and events. |
| 相対年代 | relative age | An older-or-younger relationship to other evidence. |
| 数値年代 | numerical age | An age expressed numerically, for example in years. |
| 暦年代 | calendar age | An age placed on a calendar timescale. |
| 時間分解能 | temporal resolution | The time interval resolved by a record. |
| 不確かさ | uncertainty | The limited certainty of a measurement or inference. |
| 放射性炭素年代測定 | radiocarbon dating | Dating based on radiocarbon in a sample. |
| 較正 | calibration | Relating radiocarbon ages to calendar ages using a calibration curve. |
| 半減期 | half-life | The time for half the atoms of a radioactive isotope to decay. |
| 層序学 | stratigraphy | The study of strata and their relationships. |
| ルミネッセンス年代測定 | luminescence dating | Dating time since mineral signals were reset by light or heat. |
| 古地磁気 | palaeomagnetism | Records of past geomagnetic fields preserved in materials. |
| 年輪年代学 | dendrochronology | The study of change using calendar-dated tree rings. |
| 代理指標 | proxy | Evidence used to infer an environmental variable indirectly. |
| 古気候学 | paleoclimatology | The study of past climates. |
| 年縞 | varve | A sedimentary layer verified to represent one year. |
